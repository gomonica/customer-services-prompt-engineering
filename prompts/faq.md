**AI Response**

If you forgot your password, you can usually reset it by following these steps:

1. Go to the sign-in page for the service.
2. Select “Forgot password?” or “Reset password.”
3. Enter the email address or username associated with your account.
4. Check your email or phone for the password-reset instructions.
5. Follow the link or enter the verification code, then create a new, unique password.
6. Sign in again using your new password.

If you don’t receive the reset message:

1. Check your spam/junk folder.
2. Make sure you entered the correct email address.
3. Wait a few minutes and request another reset link.
4. If available, use another verification method such as a recovery phone number.

Security reminder: Never share your password or verification/reset code with anyone. Make sure the reset link comes from the service’s official website or app.

If you tell me which service or app you’re trying to access, I can give you the exact reset steps
