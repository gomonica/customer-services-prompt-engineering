# FAQ Prompt Evaluation

## Test Case 1 : Change Password
### Version 1

## Customer Input

I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

## AI Response
If you forgot your password, you can usually reset it by selecting “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page and following the instructions sent to your registered email address. If you don’t receive the reset email, please check your spam or junk folder. If you still can’t reset your password, contact customer support for further assistance. Thank you for your patience and understanding.

## What is good?
- good instruction
- Clear & Helpfull
- easy to understand
- provide an alternatif solution
  
## What is missing?
- No security reminder 
- Assumes email is used 
- The closing greeting is not appropriate
