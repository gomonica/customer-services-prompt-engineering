# Shipping Prompt Evaluation

## Prompt Version

Version 1

## Customer Input
Could you please check the shipping status of my order?  
## AI Response
Of course. Please provide your tracking number (and the carrier, if you know it), and I’ll help check the shipping status.

## What is good?
- Polite
- Clear
- Helpfull
  
## What is missing?
- No greetings
- information the order date (if the tracking number isn't available)
- No explanation tracking number is needed

## Score
Accuracy: 4/5
Completeness: 4/5
Tone & Professionalism : 4/5
Overall: 4/5

## Improvement
prompts should begin with a friendly greeting and acknowledgment of the customer’s request. then ask for the necessary tracking details, such as the tracking number, order date, and carrier. The response should also briefly explain that these details are needed to check the shipping status accurately.
