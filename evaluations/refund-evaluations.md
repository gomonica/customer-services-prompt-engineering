# Refund Prompt Evaluation

## Prompt Version

Version 1

## Customer Input
I bought a dress yesterday, but it's torn at the edge. I'd like to get a refund.
## AI Response
I'd be glad to help. Since you purchased the dress yesterday and it arrived with a tear at the edge, it may be eligible for a refund under the store's policy for defective items.

To process your request, I'll need your order number or proof of purchase.

In the meantime, if possible:

Keep the dress in its current condition.
If you can, take a few clear photos showing the tear, as they may be requested during the review.
Once you share your order number (or receipt details), I can help you with the next steps for your refund request.

## What is good?
- Polite
- Clear
- Helpfull
## What is missing?
- Lack of empathy
- The refund policy is too vague
- No explanation of the refund process
## Improvement
prompts should show more empathy, appreciation for contacting support and explain the refund process 

Version 2 performs better because it:
- asks for order number
- explains refund timeline
- shows empathy
- appreciation customer for contacting
